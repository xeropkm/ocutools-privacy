Privacy Policy for OcuTools

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OcuTools ("the App"), developed by Ocular ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

OcuTools does not require account registration, login, or the submission of any personal information to use its core features. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Photos or videos captured by the QR scanner (the camera is used only to detect QR codes in real time; no images are saved or transmitted)
  • Compass, level, or other sensor readings (used only locally to display real-time results on your device)
  • Content you enter to generate QR codes (processed entirely on your device)

2. Permissions Used by the App

  • Camera — used solely for the QR code scanning feature. No images or video are stored or uploaded.
  • Motion and orientation sensors — used solely for the Compass and Level features. Sensor data stays on your device and is never transmitted.

3. Advertising

OcuTools displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information (such as your advertising ID, device information, and general location) to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device's settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

4. Third-Party Services

OcuTools uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements

These third parties may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for their data practices.

5. Data Retention

Since OcuTools does not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no user data retained by us. Any data processed by third-party services (such as AdMob) is retained according to their own policies.

6. Children's Privacy

OcuTools is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
